---
title: "Dunning Cascade"
description: "Automatic payment recovery with escalating retry strategies"
---

When a payment fails, RailSwitch activates the **dunning cascade** — an escalating sequence of retry strategies designed to maximize payment recovery.

## Cascade Stages

| Stage | Strategy | Timing |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Retry existing card | Immediate |
| 2 | Retry existing card + notify | 30 minutes |
| 3 | Retry existing card + notify | 2 hours |
| 4 | Retry existing card + notify | 6 hours |
| 5 | Email customer (card expired?) | 12 hours |
| 6 | SMS customer | 24 hours |
| 7 | Fallback to Virtual Account if available | 48 hours |
| 8 | Final notice | 72 hours |
| 9 | Mark as exhausted | 96 hours |

## How It Works

1. A charge attempt fails (insufficient funds, expired card, etc.)
2. The invoice enters `past_due` status
3. The dunning cascade begins at stage 1
4. Each stage triggers the configured action
5. If a payment succeeds at any stage, the cascade stops and the invoice is marked `paid`
6. If all stages are exhausted, the subscription enters `unpaid` status

## Webhook Events

The cascade emits these events:

| Event | When |
|-------|------|
| `dunning.cascade_started` | First failure triggers the cascade |
| `dunning.fallback_initiated` | Fallback payment method is attempted |
| `dunning.exhausted` | All retry attempts failed |

## Configuring the Cascade

Cascade settings can be configured in the [Dashboard](https://railswitch-gateway.fly.dev) under Settings → Dunning.
